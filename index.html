Repair-Priority Guided Endpoint Flow Postfilter for Noise-Robust Neural Speech Codecs

Overview

Noise-robust neural speech codecs often suffer from perceptual degradation at low bitrates and low signal-to-noise ratios. While generative postfilters can improve quality, they typically perform uniform global restoration, ignoring perceptually critical regions that remain severely distorted. In this letter, we propose FlowRep, a repair-priority guided endpoint flow postfilter for noise-robust speech codecs. Specifically, a Repair-Priority Estimator predicts a priority map to reweight the training objective, guiding the flow model to focus on regions requiring critical restoration. Furthermore, we formulate post-enhancement as endpoint flow matching. Through direct prediction of the clean spectral target from intermediate states, this formulation naturally accommodates metric-oriented objectives and provides a flexible quality-complexity trade-off via adjustable inference steps. Experimental results demonstrate that our method significantly improves the perceptual quality and intelligibility of codec-decoded speech, especially under challenging low-SNR conditions.
